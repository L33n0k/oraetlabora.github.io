Ora et labora, Finish first.

Ora et Labora is a dedicated reading room, not a café and not a coaching class. People come here to open a book and stay with it for hours.

Every seat is a private compartment with its own reading light, charging socket and storage. Phones stay silent, conversation stays outside, and the rules are simple so the room stays calm for everyone.

It suits exam aspirants, college students, researchers and professionals who need a place to think. It is four minutes from the highway, with a washroom attached and a cafeteria in the same building.

What your seat includes

Everything is set up so you can sit down and start.

Private compartments

Your own desk with side panels, so you see the page and nothing else.

Storage and lighting

Shelves in each compartment for your books, with a reading light at the desk.

Charging sockets

A socket within reach of every seat for your laptop and phone.

Fully air-conditioned

Cool, ventilated rooms with natural light from high windows.

Books and newspaper

An open library of general reading, plus the daily newspaper.

Wi-Fi for study

Available to members for academic use. Streaming and gaming are not allowed.

Silent study areas

Phones on silent and no eating inside, so the rooms stay quiet.

Washroom and cafeteria

Washroom attached. Refreshments on the ground floor, in the same building.

CCTV monitored

The centre is under CCTV. Keep your belongings with you or stored securely.

Plans and shifts

Choose a half-day shift or the full day, then pay by the day, week, month or year. The longer the plan, the less each day costs.

One-time registration is ₹500 for every plan. Seats are limited, so reserve early.

House rules

A short list that keeps the room quiet for everyone.

Entry and conduct
  • Show valid ID when you enter. Non-members cannot enter.
  • Keep phones off or on silent in study areas.
  • No rude, disruptive or disrespectful behaviour toward staff or other members.
Space and cleanliness
  • Keep your compartment and the restroom clean.
  • No eating, drinking or littering in the study areas. Use the ground floor for refreshments.
Wi-Fi, safety and membership
  • Wi-Fi is for academic use only: no illegal downloads, prohibited sites, streaming or gaming.
  • The centre is CCTV secured but is not responsible for loss of or damage to belongings.
  • Breaking the rules can lead to a warning or the end of membership. Terms may be updated at any time.
